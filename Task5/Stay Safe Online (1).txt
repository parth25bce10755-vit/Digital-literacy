 Stay Safe Online: Checklist for College Students (India)
* Use strong, unique passwords:
Create different passwords for each account and include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid using easily guessed details like your name or birthdate. 
* Enable two-factor authentication (2FA):
Turn on 2FA for apps like WhatsApp and Instagram. This adds an extra layer of security beyond just your password. 
* Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks:
Do not access banking or personal accounts on unsecured public networks. Hackers can intercept your data on such connections. 
* Beware of phishing links and messages:
Do not click on suspicious links received via email, SMS, or social media. Always verify the sender before taking any action. 
* Secure your UPI transactions:
Double-check the receiver's name before sending money through UPI apps like Google Pay or PhonePe. Even a small mistake can send money to the wrong person. 
* Never share OTP or UPI PIN:
Banks or apps will never ask for your OTP or PIN. Sharing these can lead to immediate financial fraud. 
* Limit app permissions:
Only allow necessary permissions (camera, location, contacts) to apps. Unnecessary access can lead to data misuse. 
* Keep devices and apps updated:
Regular updates fix security vulnerabilities. Always install updates from official sources like app stores. 
* Log out from shared devices:
Always sign out of accounts when using college computers or cyber cafés. This prevents unauthorized access.
