Checklist: Responsible Social Media Use for College Students
✅ Do's
* Think before posting: Make sure your content is respectful and appropriate.
* Protect your privacy: Use strong privacy settings on apps like Instagram.
* Verify information: Check facts before sharing any news or updates.
* Be respectful: Communicate politely and avoid offensive language.
* Use it productively: Follow educational content and build professional networks.
❌ Don'ts
* Don't overshare: Avoid posting personal details like your address or phone number.
* Don't argue online: Stay away from unnecessary conflicts or heated debates.
* Don't post inappropriate content: Avoid anything offensive or harmful.
* Don't click unknown links: They may lead to scams or malware.
* Don't ignore your digital footprint: Remember that your posts can affect your future.
